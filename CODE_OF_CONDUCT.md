# Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in TabTS-SkillBench to be safe, constructive, and
welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, methods, and code without personal attacks.
- Respect differing backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect confidential reports, private data, and unpublished results.
- Correct mistakes transparently, including benchmark or evaluation errors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these
expectations. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and
protect the privacy of reporters.

The project must publish a private maintainer contact before v1.0.0. Until then,
do not include sensitive vulnerability details in a public issue; follow the
temporary reporting guidance in `SECURITY.md`.
